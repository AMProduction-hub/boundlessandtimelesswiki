# Low-Light Senses
*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 326*  

**Prerequisite**: Sewer-Dwelling Creature

The sewer creature gains [blindsight](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/senses.md#Blindsight) or [darkvision](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/senses.md#Darkvision) out to a range of 60 feet.
