# Liquefy
*3rd-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when hit with an attack by a creature within 5 feet of you

**Range:** 5 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

## Modes

***3rd-Order.*** You psionically barrage your attacker with transformative energy. The attacker must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be pushed 5 feet away from you and [restrained](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Restrained) until the end of their next turn, as their lower body temporarily melts into an immovable goo.

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 392*
