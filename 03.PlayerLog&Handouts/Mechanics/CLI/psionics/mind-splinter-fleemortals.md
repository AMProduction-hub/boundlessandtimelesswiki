# Mind Splinter
*1st-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

## Modes

***1st-4th Level.*** You attempt to erase your image from the mind of a creature within range. Make a ranged power attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `1d6` psychic damage, and you become [invisible](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) to them until the start of your next turn.

***5th-10th Level.*** You attempt to erase your image from the mind of a creature within range. Make a ranged power attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `2d6` psychic damage, and you become [invisible](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) to them until the start of your next turn.

***11th-16th Level.*** You attempt to erase your image from the mind of a creature within range. Make a ranged power attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `3d6` psychic damage, and you become [invisible](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) to them until the start of your next turn.

***17th+ Level.*** You attempt to erase your image from the mind of a creature within range. Make a ranged power attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `4d6` psychic damage, and you become [invisible](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Invisible) to them until the start of your next turn.

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 392*
