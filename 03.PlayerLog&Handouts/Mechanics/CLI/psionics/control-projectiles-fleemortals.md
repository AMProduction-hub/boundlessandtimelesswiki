# Control Projectiles
*2nd-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when a creature you can see within 60 feet of you makes a ranged weapon attack

**Range:** 60 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

## Modes

***2nd-Order.*** You telekinetically affect the projectile's flight. The attack roll is made with advantage or disadvantage (your choice).

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 391*
