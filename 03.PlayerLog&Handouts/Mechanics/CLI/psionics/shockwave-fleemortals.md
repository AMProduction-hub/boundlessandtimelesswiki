# Shockwave
*2nd-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

## Modes

***2nd-Order.*** You unleash a psionic shock wave. Each creature within 10 feet of you must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed up to 10 feet directly away from you.

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 393*
