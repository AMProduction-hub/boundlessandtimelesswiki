# Memory Thief
*4th-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 feet

## Modes

***4th-Order (conc., 1 min).*** You plunder the mind of a creature you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw. On a failed save, the target takes `4d10` psychic damage, their proficiency bonus is lowered by 1, and you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and your power save DC for the duration.

***Increased Order.*** When you manifest this power, you can increase its order by 1 or more. For each increase of 1, the damage increases by `2d10`.

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 392*
