# Sickening Sympathy
*2nd-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 reaction, which you take when another creature you can see within 30 feet of you is hit with an attack

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** 1 round

## Modes

***2nd-Order.*** The creature who made the triggering attack must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or be [poisoned](03.PlayerLog&Handouts/Mechanics/CLI/rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) until the end of their next turn as they feel the nauseous pain of their target.

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 393*
