# Invigorated March
*3rd-Order*

**Manifestation Time:** 1 bonus action

**Range:** 30 feet

**Duration:** Instantaneous

## Modes

***3rd-Order.*** You emit a burst of mentally invigorating energy into the mind of a creature you can see within range. That creature can use their reaction, if available, to move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

***Increased Order.*** When you manifest this power, you can increase its order by 1 or more. For each increase of 1, you target one additional creature.

*Source: Flee, Mortals! p. 391*
