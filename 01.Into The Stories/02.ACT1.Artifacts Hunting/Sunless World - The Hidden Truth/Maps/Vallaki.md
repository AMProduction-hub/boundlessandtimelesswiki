# Vallaki

## Overview

**Type:** City **Current Monarch:** Bartolomeus Vallaki XIII — Crown Prince 
**Population:** Approximately 3,000 mortals, 400 registered vampires 
**Status:** Under lockdown — Festival of Vallaki's Royal Lineage in T=8 hours

Vallaki is the largest settlement in Barovia and the closest thing the plane has to a functioning city. It operates under a coexistence compact between mortals and vampires — enforced through the registration system, the blood tax, and the wrist-mark infrastructure maintained in the tunnels beneath the city by [[ADAM|WARGA LOKAL]].

The city appears functional. It is functional. The horror is not that something is wrong on the surface. The horror is what the surface is built on.

## Districts

### The Palace / Vallaki Castle Area

Home to the Crown Prince Bartolomeus Vallaki XIII and the royal administration. The Festival of Vallaki's Royal Lineage opens here. The Black Hood Society operates through the royal family without the Crown Prince's full awareness of the extent of their influence — he knows he is managed, he does not know by whom entirely.

The Castle library holds the Black Hood Society's Vallaki compliance records — the documentation of which noble families are under leverage and how. Adrianna Vegelberg ([[Alexander]]) is currently inside attempting to destroy these records.

**Current occupants of note:**

- The Crown Prince
- [[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] — attending the Festival gathering
- [[Minerva]] — performing, then investigating with [[HAWA]]
- Adrianna/Alexander — running her operation as ballet cover
- Sasha Vegelberg — running Black Hood counter-intelligence
- [[Asep]] — assigned to guard detail

### Upper City / Grand Boulevard

Noble district. Mademoiselle's Cabaret Show operates here — the intelligence and leverage operation that feeds information to the Thieves Guild. The upper class conducts its private gatherings in the establishments along the Grand Boulevard, including the kind that requires a specific invitation and a willingness not to ask questions about certain things.

**Locations of note:**

- Mademoiselle's Opera Theatre — Grand Boulevard, exact address kept discreet
- The Grand Boulevard Café — where [[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] wrote _"Stone located"_ in his notebook

### Middle City / Uptown

Middle class residential. The eastern service tunnel ADAM showed [[Sephire]] surfaces in a maintenance cellar beneath an Uptown building. Registration compliance is highest here — the middle class has the most to lose from the system breaking.

### Market Area / Central City

Commercial district. The Silent Inn is located here — the establishment with a sign language only policy and HAWA's basement safehouse beneath it. The party's base of operations for the Barovia arc.

**Locations of note:**

- **The Silent Inn** — sign language only in the common room; spoken word permitted in HAWA's basement safehouse below
    - HAWA's investigation board is here
    - The [[Deep Purple Netherstone]] is on HAWA's desk
    - Three fading vampires are currently sheltering here with [[Verdian Suyanti|Venty]]

### Lower City

Working class district. The Thieves Guild operates here. The four whale oil lampposts that mark the Guild's entrance — as opposed to the city's standard magical lighting — are located in this district. The Loudest Inn is here.

**Locations of note:**

- **The Loudest Inn** — Steven the Dwarf's establishment. Two dead Githyanki were disposed of from the second floor window. Steven has been directing people to the Guild for years.
- **Whale oil lampposts (x4)** — the fourth one has a button at its base that opens a stairway leading down into the Guild's tunnel network
- **The Thieves Guild Cistern** — accessible through the tunnel network beneath the Lower City; the Guild's nerve center
- **The Drainage Tunnels** — extends throughout the Lower City and connects to ADAM's private tunnel network without the Guild's knowledge

## The Tunnel Network (Hidden — DM Reference)

Beneath Vallaki lies an infrastructure that predates the city itself. ADAM has been using and maintaining it for decades.

**Known entry points:**

- The Silent Inn basement → Central City movement corridor
- Eastern wall service tunnel → Uptown (shown to [[Sephire]] in Session 25)
- Whale oil lamppost hatch → Guild's sewer entry (Session 26 — Froggo and Kairos)
- ADAM's private access → directly beneath Vallaki Castle's foundation

**The registration infrastructure:** The physical mechanism of the wrist-mark system is located in the deeper tunnels — old Netherese construction, maintained by ADAM personally. This is what ADAM has been doing for a hundred years. Not just watching. Maintaining.

If the party finds this — they find the proof that the system is real, deliberate, and maintained by one person who chose to stay.

**Guild tunnels vs ADAM's tunnels:** The Guild's sewer network runs parallel to ADAM's private network. They never intersect. ADAM designed the separation deliberately. He has been protecting the Guild's operation without their knowledge for years.

## The Festival of Vallaki's Royal Lineage

**Occurs at T=0 — the Festival opening ceremony.**

An annual celebration of the Vallaki royal bloodline. Mortals and vampires alike attend — it is one of the few public events where both groups are present in the streets simultaneously. The registered vampires participate in good faith. The compact has held for a hundred years.

The Black Hood Society operates the Festival's security and logistics through the Crown Prince's administration without the Crown Prince's full knowledge.

Mademoiselle and Alexander have been planning their Festival operation for three years — the one night every Black Hood operative is publicly visible.

[[HAWA]] plans to use the Stone during the opening ceremony to call Selûne's moonlight down on Vallaki as a blessing.

The Festival is not the threat. The Festival is when every threat becomes simultaneous.

## Background

Vallaki has been a managed city since Strahd's disappearance a hundred years ago. The coexistence compact is not idealistic — it is pragmatic. Mortals get protection from unregistered vampires. Registered vampires get sanctioned feeding schedules and legal status. Everyone lives under rules nobody chose but everyone has come to rely on.

The Black Hood Society entered this arrangement approximately 80 years ago, positioning themselves as the administrative backbone of the compact's enforcement. The Crown Prince's bloodline has been their preferred instrument since the second generation.

Nobody in Vallaki — mortal or vampire — knows the compact runs on the [[Deep Purple Netherstone]] that [[HAWA]] has been carrying in her bag for six months.
