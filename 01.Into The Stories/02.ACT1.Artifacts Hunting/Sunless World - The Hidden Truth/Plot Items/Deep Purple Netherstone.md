# Deep Purple Netherese Stone

## Overview

**Type:** Artifact — Netherese Origin **Group:** Wondrous Items **Rarity:** Unique **Attunement:** None required

![[z_Assets/Misc/ImagePlaceholder.png|Placeholder Picture]]

The Deep Purple Netherese Stone is a smooth, fist-sized stone of deep violet hue with faint silver veining that pulses slowly — like a heartbeat, or a tide. It is warm to the touch regardless of ambient temperature. It has no visible seams, markings, or activating mechanisms.

It was recovered from beneath Castle Ravenloft by [[HAWA]] six months before the party's arrival in Barovia.

---

## The Layered Truth

_This item has multiple layers of truth. Reveal them to players in sequence as they investigate._

### Layer 1 — What It Appears To Be

A Netherese communication artifact. Functions similarly to a [[commune|Commune]] spell — capable of reaching across planes to contact a divine or powerful being. Bureau of Time and Plane designation: _Artifact 5 of 6, Barovia retrieval._

### Layer 2 — What It Actually Does

The Stone does not merely communicate with a divine being. It communes _through_ one. Specifically: it interfaces directly with Selûne's physical manifestation in Barovia — the moon above the plane — and can issue commands to it.

The moon above Barovia is not a natural satellite. It is Selûne's anchored body, fixed in this plane by the Stone's signal. The moon passes twice daily because it tracks the Stone's physical location — wherever the Stone goes, the moon follows.

**Evidence the players can find:**

- The moon's path correlates precisely with [[HAWA]]'s daily movements through Vallaki
- Registered vampires' wrist-marks are fading fastest in districts where [[HAWA]] walks most frequently
- The _'Guide of Selune Bodies Manipulation'_ recovered from Castle Ravenloft's library describes this exact mechanism

### Layer 3 — What It Is

**The Stone is [[Strahd von Zarovich]].**

Strahd did not die. He did not flee. He transformed — converting his physical form into a Netherese-compatible stone object as a final act of self-preservation, or perhaps as a deliberate strategy. A hundred years of isolation, a hundred years of a domain slowly adapting without him.

The registration system, the blood tax, the coexistence compact — all of it runs off the Stone's signal. The signal is Strahd's transformed consciousness, passively maintaining the order he built.

**[[ADAM|WARGA LOKAL]] knows this.** He has known for decades. He is maintaining his father's domain while his father sleeps inside an artifact that [[HAWA]] carries in her bag.

### Layer 4 — What Ao Wants With It

The Bureau of Time and Plane's mission is to collect six artifacts for reasons the party were not fully briefed on. The true purpose: Ao intends to use the six stones to absorb the power of every god and goddess whose influence is bound within them.

The Stone contains Strahd's transformed power — which includes a century of ambient divine authority accumulated from ruling a demiplane anchored to Selûne herself.

Taking the Stone to the Bureau completes Ao's plan. It also:

- Releases the moon from its anchor — Selûne's body, unmoored
- Collapses the registration system
- Potentially wakes Strahd in a context where he has no domain and no control
- Adds his accumulated power to Ao's collection alongside the other five artifacts

**[[Kairos]] knows Ao's plan.** He was told by Damian on the Pequod after Shattar-Kai. He has said nothing.

## Current Status

The Stone is currently in [[HAWA]]'s possession at her basement safehouse beneath The Silent Inn, Vallaki.

[[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] has written _"Stone located"_ in his private notebook. The Black Hood Society's response team is mobilizing.

[[HAWA]] has read the _Guide of Selune Bodies Manipulation_ and believes she now understands how to use the Stone properly. She plans to use it at the Festival opening ceremony to call Selûne's moonlight down on Vallaki as a blessing.

She does not know she will be yanking on Selûne's anchor chain. She does not know the Stone is Strahd.

---

## Description

The Stone is unremarkable to look at. Smooth. Warm. Slightly too heavy for its size. The silver veining pulses with a rhythm that, if you watch it long enough, matches the moon's position in the sky rather than any natural heartbeat.

When held by a Selûnite — specifically [[HAWA]] — the pulse quickens slightly. Like recognition. Like response.

When [[Sephire]] held it briefly during the initial Bureau briefing, it did nothing. No response. No recognition. The Stone is particular about who it responds to.

## Mechanical Properties

**Commune (Modified):** Once per day, the holder can cast _Commune_ as if it were a 5th level spell. The entity contacted is always Selûne — or more accurately, the fragment of Selûne's consciousness that has been anchored to this plane for a century. Her answers are more limited and more exhausted than standard Commune results. She answers in fragmented impressions rather than clear yes/no responses.

**Moon Command (Hidden — requires _Guide of Selune Bodies Manipulation_ to unlock):** The holder can issue a direct positional command to the moon above Barovia. The moon obeys. This is not a blessing. This is a leash.

**Registration Anchor:** As long as the Stone exists and broadcasts its signal, Barovia's vampire registration system functions. Wrist-marks remain stable. Blood tax infrastructure operates. If the Stone's signal is disrupted — by misuse, destruction, or removal from the plane — the registration system begins failing within hours.

**Fading Marks:** The Stone has been out of its original location for six months. The registration signal has been gradually weakening. Vampires in Vallaki are beginning to notice their wrist-marks fading. This will accelerate as the Festival approaches.

## Construction

Netherese. Pre-Sundering. The specific mage who constructed it is unknown. The purpose at construction was likely divine communication infrastructure — a tool for managing relationships between mortal institutions and divine anchors.

Whatever it was built for, Strahd repurposed it during the final years of his rule. The modification that converted it into a registration anchor appears to have been made approximately 105 years ago — shortly before Strahd's disappearance.

The modification that converted it into Strahd's prison was made by Strahd himself.

## Additional Details

- The Stone cannot be destroyed by conventional means. It is an artifact.
- If brought to the Bureau of Time and Plane, Ao's agents will know what to do with it. The party will not be told what that is.
- [[ADAM|WARGA LOKAL]] will not allow it to leave Barovia willingly. He has contingency plans.
- If [[HAWA]] uses it as planned at the Festival — calling Selûne's moonbeam down on Vallaki — the Stone will be intact afterward. The damage will not come from the Stone breaking. It will come from it working exactly as commanded.
