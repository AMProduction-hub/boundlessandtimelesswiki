# Radovan Czeslav Voss

## Overview

Radovan Czeslav Voss is a private investigator working for the Black Hood Society. He is in Vallaki to investigate the Castle Ravenloft incident — the dome breaking, the outsider ship at Lake Zarovich, the destabilization events that have been accumulating for six months.

He is not a villain. He is a function. The function of documentation.

He does not need [[HAWA]] to be guilty of anything. He needs her to be _located._ He has located her. He wrote _"Stone located"_ in his notebook this morning. Everything above that is above his station and he is comfortable with that division of responsibility.

He is warm. He is precise. He is genuinely curious about remarkable things. He is the most dangerous man in Vallaki right now, and he knows it, and he drinks his coffee calmly regardless.

## Plot Web

**Black Hood Society — Senior Investigator**

- Not a field operative. An investigator. He documents; others act.
- His report is nearly complete. Once submitted, the Black Hood response team mobilizes.
- He outranks the Crown Prince within the Black Hood structure. The Crown Prince defers to him.
- He does not lead the response team. He hands them a complete dossier and goes home.

**Persons of Interest**

- [[Verdian Suyanti|Venty]] — tested at The Silent Inn. Poker face observed. Filed as _aware of the elf woman, protective, non-hostile._
- [[Minerva]] — _remarkable abilities, outsider, connected to Moon Reader, arrived same window as party._ Person of Interest.
- Mademoiselle — _information broker, Thieves Guild adjacent, not directly relevant to Castle Ravenloft investigation._
- The Pequod's crew — _ship identified, outsider origin confirmed._

**Suspects**

- [[HAWA]] — Primary suspect. Moon tracks her. Stone located at her safehouse. Selûnite signature on dome destruction. Case is complete. Report pending.
- Alexander — _moniker name, Thieves Guild leader, not directly relevant to Castle investigation._

**The Notebook**

- Radovan carries a small leather-bound notebook at all times.
- He has a second identical notebook. If the first is taken he produces the second without comment.
- If anyone sees even one page — _Stone located_ in precise handwriting — the picture changes.

## Personal Traits

- Speaks calmly. Uses warmth as an investigative tool rather than a social one.
- Prominent in Vallaki's upper society — present, visible, unthreatening by design.
- Acts overly warm toward suspects, genuinely caring toward non-suspects, carefully curious toward persons of interest.
- Has the patience of someone who has been doing this for a very long time and has learned that urgency is a mistake.
- Never acts without complete documentation. This is a professional standard, not a personal quirk.

## Beliefs & Ideology

_(Left deliberately sparse — Radovan is defined by method, not conviction)_

- Complete information before action. Always.

## GM Notes (Hidden)

**His report:** Radovan's report to the Black Hood Society is effectively complete. He is waiting to submit it until the Festival — so that his report and the response team's mobilization arrive simultaneously, giving [[HAWA]] no window to move the Stone. This is professional precision, not hesitation.

**He already knows:** The Stone is at The Silent Inn basement. He wrote _"Stone located"_ in his notebook before the session began. He is not searching anymore. He is timing.

**He outranks the Crown Prince:** This is visible if any player watches their interaction carefully — DC 15 Insight. The Crown Prince defers. This has been the case for years. The Crown Prince knows he is a Black Hood puppet and has accepted it.

**What he does when confronted:** He smiles. He asks questions. He documents the confrontation. He does not become hostile unless physically attacked — and even then he responds with calculated rather than emotional violence. His job is information, not enforcement. Enforcement is someone else's department.

**His one genuine reaction:** If anyone shows him evidence that the Stone is Strahd von Zarovich — he goes very still. Not afraid. Recalibrating. This is the one piece of information that would make him pause before submitting his report. Not because it changes his job. Because it changes the magnitude of the situation and he is professional enough to ensure his report reflects accurate magnitude.

**He is a vampire but does not advertise it.** He operates in daylight using shaded carriages and controlled exposure. His vampiric nature is an investigative advantage — the patience, the memory, the ability to observe without being observed. He has been in Barovia for longer than the party has been alive.
