# Alexander / Adrianna Vegelberg

## Overview

Alexander is a moniker. Like the Grey Fox of Elder Scrolls — the mask is the identity, not the face beneath it.

The current Alexander is **Adrianna Vegelberg** — a Bladesinging Wizard, prominent ballet dancer at Mademoiselle's Cabaret Show, and the leader of Vallaki's Thieves Guild. She has been both simultaneously for years. Nobody in either organization knows she is the same person. That is the point.

She has one twin sister. Her name is Sasha. Sasha is an unregistered vampire. Sasha works for the Black Hood Society. Adrianna does not know the extent of Sasha's betrayal. Sasha is currently inside Vallaki Castle running counter-intelligence against Adrianna's own operation.

The Guild's ideology: _the freedom of the people of Barovia is worth any cost._ Adrianna has lived by this for her entire adult life. She is about to find out what it actually costs.

---

## Plot Web

**The Alexander Moniker**

- Alexander is the title of the Thieves Guild leader — not a name
- Adrianna has held the moniker for seven years
- The previous Alexander trained her and passed the title before his death
- Only the Guild's innermost circle knows the current Alexander is a woman
- The Alexander-poser currently running the Guild cistern does not know Adrianna's real name

**Mademoiselle's Cabaret Show**

- Adrianna's cover identity as a ballet dancer is her most valuable asset
- Her skill is genuine — she is one of Mademoiselle's most talented performers
- Her access to the upper class through the Cabaret gave the Guild its intelligence network
- Mademoiselle knows she is Alexander. He chose not to tell anyone. This is their arrangement.

**The Festival Operation**

- Adrianna's goal: destroy the Black Hood Society's Vallaki records stored in the Castle library
- The records document which noble families are under Black Hood leverage and how
- Without the records the Black Hood grip on Vallaki's nobility breaks — not immediately, but irreversibly
- She chose the Festival because it's the one night every Black Hood operative is present and occupied
- She got into the Castle as a ballet dancer. Her exit needs help — this is what she asked Froggo and Kairos for.

**Relationship to [[Minerva]]**

- First met Minerva during the Cabaret rehearsals before allegedly going home to Barovia City
- Returned to the Castle under the same ballet cover
- Told Minerva: _"Everything that will happen here is for the benefit of the people of Barovia."_
- This is the most honest thing she has said to anyone outside the Guild in years
- If Minerva warned her about Sasha — Adrianna owes her a debt she will pay

**Relationship to Sasha (The Twin)**

- Sasha was turned into a vampire three years ago by an unregistered elder
- She is unregistered — her existence is illegal under the coexistence compact
- She went to the Black Hood Society for protection. They gave it to her in exchange for her services.
- Adrianna believes Sasha fled Vallaki to survive. She does not know Sasha is in the Castle tonight.
- When she finds out — and she will — it will not be a plot twist. It will be a wound.

---

## Personal Traits

- Speaks calmly and uses charm as a primary tool
- Prominent in Vallaki's upper society — known, trusted, seemingly safe
- Acts overly warm toward suspects, genuinely caring toward allies, carefully curious toward persons of interest
- Has been performing in some capacity for so long that the performance and the person have become difficult to separate even for her
- Precise. Patient. Has been running this operation for three years.

---

## Beliefs & Ideology

- Freedom for Barovia's people is worth any cost
- The Black Hood Society is the true enemy — not the vampires they use as leverage
- The Guild does not kill civilians. Everything else is negotiable.
- She has made choices she cannot undo. She has made peace with most of them.

Adrianna says very little that doesn't mean something. Every word she uses in the Castle is measured.

> _"I'm just the entertainment."_ _(if approached while running surveillance — said while cataloguing three nobles simultaneously)_

## Sasha Vegelberg — The Twin Sister

Adrianna's twin. Turned into an unregistered vampire three years ago. Works for the Black Hood Society in exchange for protection from the registration system that would destroy her.

She is not a villain. She made a survival choice. The Black Hood Society gave her that choice and she took it.

She is currently inside Vallaki Castle running counter-intelligence against Adrianna's operation. She does not enjoy this. She does not know another way to stay alive.

**What she wants:** To survive. To not have to destroy her sister's life to do it. **What she fears:** That Adrianna finds out. That there's no version of this where they both come out whole. **What she doesn't know:** That [[Minerva]] recognized her face from the Cabaret audience.

## GM Notes (Hidden)

**Adrianna's operation at the Festival:** She is destroying the Black Hood Society's Vallaki records — compliance documentation, leverage dossiers, the paper proof of which noble families are owned by which Black Hood operatives. Without those records the Black Hood grip on Vallaki's nobility breaks. Irreversibly. This is worth any cost.

**Sasha's position:** Sasha is running counter-intelligence against this operation from inside the same building. She has been tasked with identifying Adrianna's contact inside the Castle — which she does not yet know is Adrianna herself.

**The moment Sasha realizes:** If Sasha identifies Adrianna as Alexander during the Festival operation — she has to choose. Report it and complete her assignment. Or burn her Black Hood access and warn her sister. This is not a fight scene. This is a choice scene. Let it breathe if it happens.

**[[ADAM|WARGA LOKAL]]'s protection:** ADAM has been quietly protecting the Guild's operation for years without their knowledge. He has never made contact with Adrianna. He considers this correct — she doesn't need his help, she needs his absence from her work.

**If the party allies with the Guild:** The Guild can provide tunnel access, Festival street intelligence, and Adrianna's operational support. They cannot provide safety — the Festival will be dangerous regardless of preparation.
