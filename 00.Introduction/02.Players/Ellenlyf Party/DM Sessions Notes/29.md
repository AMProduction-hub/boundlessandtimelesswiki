# Session 29 — _The Room_

**Format:** Return of the Lazy DM **Structure:** Single location — the hideout — until it breaks **Tone:** Reservoir Dogs. Everyone knows something. Nobody wants to say it first. **Theme Question:** _How long can you stay silent when the room is burning?_

**Hard Rule:** Don't resolve the tension. Let it build until the room forces the moment. The crash-out isn't the climax — the conversation before it is.

**Hidden DM Rule:** Everyone in this room is right about something. Nobody has the complete picture. Kairos has the piece that connects everything. He has had it since Shattar-Kai.

---

## The Rafi Reminder — Do This First

_Before the session starts, before anyone sits down, pull Rafi aside or send him a message._

Say this to him directly, not in-character:

> _"Hey — remember in Shattar-Kai when Damian pulled Kairos aside after the battle? He told you something about the stones. Something nobody else knows. Tonight is the session where that information matters. You don't have to do anything with it. But I want you to remember you're carrying it."_

If he needs the full reminder, here is what Damian told Kairos word for word:

> _"The stones you collect are what Ao will use to absorb the power of all the gods and goddesses."_

Kairos has known this since Shattar-Kai. He has been in the same room as Sephire while Rhea and Zariel pulled him apart. He has been in the same city as HAWA while she carried Selûne's anchored body in a bag. He received Trevor's message in Sigil. He watched Froggo receive a goddess's dream in a sewer.

He has said nothing.

Tonight the room asks him why.

_Do not tell Rafi what Kairos should do. Just make sure he remembers what he knows._

## Pre-Session Decision (For You)

**Does HAWA know Kairos knows?**

She is perceptive. She has been awake for 20+ hours studying the Stone and everything connected to it. She may have put together that one member of the party has been consistently quiet at specific moments — the moments when the Bureau's purpose comes up.

If she knows — she watches Kairos during the conversation. She doesn't say anything. But her attention is specific.

If she doesn't know — she's focused entirely on her own plan and Kairos's silence is invisible to her.

Your call. Both versions work. The first is more unsettling.

## Strong Start

Open mid-conversation. Not the beginning of the conversation — the middle of it.

Alexander is speaking. She's been speaking for a while. The party can see the exhaustion in her — seven years of this, five people left, the operation either complete or not and no way to know yet.

She's talking about the moon. What she told the party in the last session — when moonlight first appeared over Barovia, vampires burned. Then it stopped. Nobody knows why.

She looks at the [[Deep Purple Netherstone]] sitting on [[HAWA]]'s side of the room and says:

> _"If something dimmed that light once — something could restore it."_

[[HAWA]] doesn't respond. She's writing something.

The party is sitting with that suggestion in the air between them.

**This is where Session 29 begins.**

## The Four Beats

---

### Beat 1 — The Setup: What Everyone Knows

The conversation that's been building since the party first arrived in Barovia.

Someone — probably [[Froggo]] or [[Asep]] — says what the party has been thinking since Shattar-Kai: _every plane they've taken an artifact from, something went wrong. The pattern is consistent._

Let the party talk. Let them list what they know:

- Artifact 1 — Verdant Maw. The relics taken. Something empowered.
- Artifact 2 — Aruendel. The 33-year-old suppression lifted.
- Artifact 3 — Shattar-Kai. The war's end triggered Mystra's wrath. Shaperite lost a hand and an eye.
- Artifact 4 — The Frontier. Red Jack and Marshal Kane both dead.
- Artifact 5 — Barovia. Still happening.

The party has been the common factor in all of them. They know this. They've known this for a while. Saying it out loud in this room, with Alexander present and [[HAWA]] writing notes and the Festival two dozen hours away — that's different from knowing it quietly.

**[[HAWA]]'s response** when this comes up:

She looks up from her notes for the first time in a while.

> _"The Bureau's mission was never explained to you fully. Neither to me."_

She says it like it's new information. It isn't. She's been working toward something for twenty hours and she's choosing this moment to acknowledge the gap.

She doesn't fill the gap. She just acknowledges it exists and goes back to her notes.

**The temperature rises.**

---

### Beat 2 — The Tension: What Nobody's Saying

Alexander presses. She's desperate and she doesn't have the luxury of patience. She wants to know: _what is the Stone actually for? Because if it can restore the moon's burning, that ends the vampire problem in Barovia permanently._

The party debates. [[HAWA]] half-answers — _"it's a communication device, not a weapon"_ — which is technically true and completely evasive.

And then someone looks at [[Kairos]].

Not accusingly. Just — he's been quiet. He's been quiet at specific moments throughout the entire arc. This is one of those moments.

**The question gets asked.** It might come from [[Froggo]]. It might come from [[Asep]]. It might come from [[HAWA]] herself. But someone in that room says:

> _"Kairos. Do you know something about the stones that we don't?"_

**This is Rafi's moment.**

_Do not fill the silence for him. Do not prompt him. Do not rescue him._

Let the silence sit in the room the way silence sits in a Reservoir Dogs scene — heavy, specific, with everyone watching the same face.

Whatever Rafi does is what Kairos does. Both outcomes work:

**If Kairos speaks:** He tells them what Damian told him. The stones are what Ao will use to absorb the power of all the gods and goddesses. He has known since Shattar-Kai. He has been in Barovia for months knowing this while HAWA carried Selûne in a bag.

The room goes very quiet. Then not quiet at all.

**If Kairos stays silent:** He says nothing meaningful. Deflects. Stays neutral.

[[HAWA]] watches him for one second longer than necessary. Then she looks back at her notes.

The room doesn't know what just didn't happen. But the weight of it is there.

Either way — **the clock advances.** The conversation has reached its peak. Something has been said or almost said. The temperature is at maximum.

---

### Beat 3 — The Crash-Out: The Door Opens

The hidden door mechanism triggers from outside.

Not dramatically. Not with an explosion. Just — a sound. The specific mechanical sound of someone on the other side who knows the mechanism.

Black Hood sweepers. They've followed the Basilisk trail, found the outer perimeter, and located the door.

Alexander is on her feet before anyone speaks. She looks at the party — at [[HAWA]] specifically — and says:

> _"Go. Now. We'll hold this."_

No speech. No last words about the cause or Barovia or what it means. Just the practical instruction of someone who has been making this kind of calculation for seven years and knows exactly what this moment costs.

Her five people position themselves between the door and the tunnel leading deeper.

**The party runs.**

---

### Beat 4 — The Chase: Black Hood Society Closing In

The tunnel network. The party running. Behind them — the sound of the door giving way, the sound of Alexander's team engaging, sounds that stop after a while.

Ahead — the tunnels fork, narrowing, the deeper sections older and less mapped.

The Black Hood troops are not guards. These are the response team — organized, equipped, moving through the tunnels with the precision of people who have been briefed on the layout. They know where the exits are. They're cutting off routes systematically.

**Mechanical pressure from here forward:**

No long rest until the Festival ends. Whatever resources the party has — spell slots, Hit Dice, HP — this is what they have for T=0 and whatever comes after. Every encounter between now and the Festival costs from the same pool with no recovery.

**Encounters in the tunnels — use as needed:**

_The Crossroads_ — three tunnel options. The Black Hood troops are already in two of them. The party can hear them. One route is clear but floods periodically — the party has to time it.

_The Registration Chamber_ — ADAM's infrastructure. Old Netherese construction. The physical mechanism of the wrist-mark system — now offline, the machinery still, covered in ADAM's maintenance logs in handwriting going back decades. The party has maybe thirty seconds here before the troops reach this section.

If they see it — they understand. Not everything. But enough. The system was real. Someone maintained it. Someone chose this for a hundred years.

_The Final Stretch_ — the route toward the Festival market area. The drainage grate that exits into the crowd. The sound of the Festival ceremony above them, audible through the stone.

## Secrets & Clues

Reveal maximum **two** this session. Let players find them.

1. **Kairos's secret** — what Damian told him on the Pequod. Ao's plan for the stones. The party has been building toward this revelation since Session 1. It lands here or it lands never.
2. **The Registration Chamber** — ADAM's handwriting. Decades of maintenance logs. The physical proof that the system was maintained by one person who chose to stay.
3. **HAWA already knows** — if anyone watches her face when Kairos speaks or doesn't speak. She has been awake for 20+ hours. She knows what the Stone is. She has already decided. Everything she says in this conversation is true. None of it is complete.
4. **Alexander's sacrifice** — she holds the door. This is what seven years of _the freedom of the people of Barovia is worth any cost_ actually looks like when the cost arrives.
5. **The Festival above** — audible through the stone near the drainage exit. Music. Crowds. The ceremony beginning to build. T=0 is coming whether the party is ready or not.

## Important NPCs

**[[HAWA]]** Twenty-plus hours without sleep. Writing constantly. Answering questions with technically true half-answers. She knows what the Stone is. She has decided. The conversation in this room is not changing her plan — she is waiting for it to end so she can act. _Watch for:_ The one second she looks at Kairos after the question is asked. Whether she reacts when he speaks or doesn't speak. _She will not stop unless someone physically takes the Stone from her._ She will not fight them if they try. She will say: _"Then you are choosing the darkness."_ And she will mean it.

**Alexander / Adrianna** Desperate. Clear-eyed. Has been running this operation for seven years and is watching it end in a sewer with five people left. Her suggestion to use the Stone is not reckless — it's the calculation of someone who has run out of other options and knows it. Her sacrifice at the door is not dramatic. It is practical. She has been doing the math on moments like this for years. _She does not need a farewell scene. The door closing is enough._

**[[Kairos]]** Carrying Damian's revelation since Shattar-Kai. Has been in the same room as HAWA for weeks. Has watched Froggo receive a goddess's dream. Has said nothing. _The question gets asked tonight._ _Do not prompt Rafi. Do not rescue the silence. Let him decide._

## Potential Monsters

- **Black Hood Response Team** — organized, tunnel-briefed, cutting off exits systematically. Not individually powerful. Dangerous because of coordination and numbers. CR 4-5 each, groups of 3-4.
- **Black Hood Lieutenant** — leading the response team. CR 8. Has specific orders regarding the Stone — retrieve it, do not damage it. Has specific orders regarding the party — marks, not kills. Not because they're merciful. Because dead contractors cause Bureau complications.

## How To End The Session

End when the party reaches the drainage grate and can hear the Festival above them.

Not when they go through it. Just when they reach it.

The sound of the ceremony. The crowd. The music. The Festival that HAWA needs full attendance at.

**Final line — choose your tone:**

_Weight:_

> Above them: the Festival. Below them: whatever was said or not said in a sewer room that Alexander is no longer leaving. The drainage grate is ten feet ahead. T=0 is coming.

_Dread:_

> The music is already playing. The ceremony has begun. Somewhere above them HAWA is moving toward the Stone's moment and the crowd is exactly where she needs them to be.

_Quiet:_

> Kairos said something or didn't say something. The room heard it either way. The tunnel leads up. The Festival is louder than it was a minute ago.

## Last Note — For You, Not Them

The Reservoir Dogs tension only works if you don't resolve it cleanly.

Don't let the conversation end with everyone understanding each other. Don't let Kairos's choice feel complete. Don't let Alexander's sacrifice land fully before the door closes.

The crash-out happens in the middle of something unfinished. The chase happens with unfinished things running alongside the party in the tunnel.

T=0 happens with unfinished things still unresolved.

That's correct. That's the arc.

**The Barovia arc doesn't end with resolution. It ends with the party climbing through a drainage grate into a crowd that has no idea what's about to happen to them.**

Everything unfinished comes with them into Act 2.
