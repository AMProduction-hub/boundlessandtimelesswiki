# Session 26 — _Path Of Righteousness Filled With Wickedness_

**Format:** Return of the Lazy DM **Structure:** Non-linear intercut POV — three simultaneous threads converging **Tone:** Compression. Every faction closing in. The city tightening around the Festival like a fist. **Theme Question:** _What does it cost to be on the right side?_

**Hard Rule:** Every scene ends with something unresolved. Nobody gets closure this session. Closure is what the Festival is for.

**Hidden DM Rule:** Every faction believes they are about to win. The Black Hoods, the Guild, HAWA, the party. They are all correct about different things and catastrophically wrong about the same thing. Nobody in this session knows what HAWA is about to do. Including HAWA.

## Strong Start

**Open on [[Radovan Czeslav Voss]]. Not on the party.**

Small table. Grand Boulevard café. Leather notebook open in front of him. He is writing with the unhurried precision of someone who has never once misspelled a word in his life.

He finishes a sentence. Reads it back. Makes one small amendment.

Closes the notebook.

Looks up at the moon.

It has crossed the sky once already this morning. It will cross again before evening. He has been tracking this for three weeks. The pattern is consistent — the moon's path correlates precisely with the movement of one specific individual through the city.

He picks up his coffee. Takes one sip. Sets it down.

Opens the notebook. Writes two words.

> _Stone located._

Closes it.

Stands up. Straightens his jacket. Checks the time.

He has a party to attend.

**Cut immediately to — The Loudest Inn, Lower City. T=74.00.**

#### Layer 2 — The Flow Redirect

T-junction. Both passages look identical. Both smell the same.

- **DC 18 Investigation** — water behavior analysis. The current moves subtly toward the correct passage if you stop and watch it.
- **DC 16 Perception** — mortar color difference on the brick at knee height on the correct wall. Too consistent to be natural aging.
- **DC 14 Perception — [[Froggo]] only, roll with Gloomstalker advantage** — woodsmoke smell venting through a crack near the brick. The Guild's fire from further in. Faint but real.

**Optional encounter — the fading vampire:**

In the wrong passage, near the staged dead end, huddled against the wall — a registered vampire. Young-looking. His wrist-mark is almost gone. He came down here two days ago when his number started fading because he didn't know where else to go.

He's not hostile. He's frightened and hungry and has been rationing the last of his self-control.

He looks at his wrist when the party finds him.

> _"Is it happening to others? I thought it was just me."_

It's not just him. This is the second data point after [[Verdian Suyanti|Venty]]'s vampire at The Silent Inn. The system is failing faster than anyone knows.

The party can help him, leave him, or take him along. No wrong answer. But the answer matters.

#### Layer 3 — The Gauge Room

Three inches of water covering the floor. A mechanical gauge mounted on a post in the center — old, worn, partially illegible markings. Three pipes emerge from the far wall at different heights. Only one leads forward. The other two flood.

- **DC 19 Investigation** — the torch reflection in the water surface forms a directional pattern pointing toward the correct pipe entrance.
- **DC 16 Insight/Psychic — [[Kairos]] only, Soulknife ability** — residual psychic impression on the gauge. He feels the deliberate weight of a hand on one specific marking. The last Guild member who used this touched it with purpose. That's the pipe.

### SCENE C — _What's Left of the Guild_

**Who:** [[Froggo]] + [[Kairos]] + [[Asep]] **Location:** The Guild cistern, beneath Lower City — T=72.00 **Tone:** A headquarters running on skeleton crew and held breath. Except it isn't really skeleton crew — most of the Guild is already above ground, invisible.

What looks like a skeleton crew is deliberate. Most of the Guild's active members are already positioned above ground, embedded in Festival preparation — invisible as vendors, decorators, food stall operators. The cistern is the nerve center, not the whole body.

The Alexander-poser understands this. He runs the nerve center with the competence of someone trained by someone excellent and who knows he's not that excellent himself. He sizes the party up immediately. Notes they haven't slept. Notes the Githyanki fight on their faces.

> _"You came through the tunnels. Steven vouched. That gets you five minutes."_

**What the Guild knows:**

**What the Guild will trade:**

> _"The dancer inside the Castle — she's been there since last night. Whatever she's doing up there, it ends at the Festival opening. After that she's done and gone."_

He doesn't know this is Alexander. He knows it's the most important asset the Guild has.

**The exhaustion matters here.** They've been awake for over 24 hours. They're being asked to commit to a Festival operation starting in roughly 22 hours. Whatever they decide they're deciding it tired.

### SCENE D — _The Correct Dress_

**Who:** [[Minerva]] **Location:** Vallaki Castle, receiving room and main hall — T=68.00 **Tone:** Beautiful. The wrongness is architectural. It lives in the sightlines and the spacing and the way attention moves through the room.

The performers wait in the receiving room. They are not guests. Guests are in the main hall. The distinction is managed carefully and never stated.

[[Minerva]] reads the room the way she reads bodies. The other performers are careful. They smile when addressed and go quiet when not. They hold themselves in the specific way of people who have learned what happens when they take up too much space in a room like this.

When she enters the main hall for the full gathering performance:

The room is wrong. The nobles aren't watching the performances. They're watching _each other watching_ the performances. Attention moves between them in a pattern — from performer to specific noble to specific other noble — like a language she doesn't speak.

She performs. Stars fill the ceiling. The crowd reacts.

And then she looks down from the performance space and sees:

**Adrianna** — near the side of the hall, in ballet position, watching the nobles rather than the performance.

**DC 16 Insight** — Adrianna isn't watching the room the way a performer watches a room. She's cataloguing. Tracking which nobles look at which other nobles when [[Minerva]] performs. Building a mental map in real time.

And across the room, near the entrance:

**The twin** — black robes, gold lining, pale. Standing in a position that gives her sightlines to both the main entrance and Adrianna simultaneously. She is not here as a guest.

They're running opposing surveillance operations in the same room.

[[Minerva]] is holding a starfield over the entire hall and she is the only person in it who has seen both their faces before.
