# Session 25 — _What Good Looks Like_

**Format:** Return of the Lazy DM **Structure:** Non-linear intercut POV **Tone:** Quiet dread under a civilized surface **Theme Question:** _Who decides what help means?_

**Hard Rule:** Cut on revelation, danger, or demanded decision. Never let one POV breathe long enough to resolve. The players don't control the cuts. You do.

**Hidden DM Rule:** Nobody learns the full picture this session. Every POV holds one piece. The tragedy is that the pieces fit — and nobody is in the same room long enough to see it.

## Pre-Session Decision (For You)

**Before you run this session, decide: where is Alexander?**

The Lower City sweep ended Session 24 actively hunting him. By Session 25 dawn one of these is true:

- **Alexander is captured** — The Thieves Guild is headless going into the Festival. Mademoiselle is now operating without his network. The Lower City is quieter, more frightened, more compliant.
- **Alexander is still running** — The Lower City is chaotic. The sweep is ongoing and aggressive. Guards are tense and making mistakes. The Thieves Guild is fractured but not gone.

The street energy in Scene C changes completely depending on your answer. Pick one before the session and let it bleed into the atmosphere.

## Strong Start

Dawn. Grey light.

**ADAM is alone at Vallaki's outer wall.**

He isn't moving. He's standing, watching the guard rotation with the patience of someone who has calculated this exact scenario many times before and found the same answer every time.

He speaks. Not to anyone. Just — speaks. Quietly, the way people talk when they've been alone long enough that silence starts to feel irresponsible.

> _"A hundred years."_

Beat.

> _"A hundred years and the mortals still walk to the market on Tuesdays. The vampires still file their feeding reports. The moon still rises."_

He watches a guard exchange happen at the eastern parapet. Two guards becoming four.

> _"And then someone arrives who wants to help."_

He hears hoofbeats on the road before he sees anything. Doesn't turn. Just listens. Counts the rhythm. One horse. Tired horse. Rider who's been in the saddle too long.

He turns when Sephire comes into view — road-worn, 18 hours from Ravenloft on his face — and looks at him the way ADAM looks at everything. Like he already knew. Like he's been waiting. Like he's already calculated what comes next and found it acceptable.

He says nothing for a long moment.

Then:

> _"You're late."_

He starts walking along the wall. Away from the gate. Away from the guards.

> _"Keep up."_

**Cut immediately — before Sephire responds — to the Loudest Inn.**

## Potential Scenes

---

### SCENE A — _The Spawn and The Castle_

**Who:** Sephire + ADAM **Location:** Castle Ravenloft interior **Tone:** Wrong kind of quiet. Something that has been waiting a very long time. **When to run:** Early in the session as immediate past — the thing both of them are carrying into the Strong Start.

They found it in one of the identical rooms lining the inner balcony. Not hiding. Not hunting. Just standing in the center of the room facing the door. Like it had been standing there for a very long time.

Strahd's spawn. What's left of one.

It is the most monstrous thing Sephire has seen in Barovia. Not because it's powerful. Because of what it used to be. Whatever humanity it carried has been hollowed out by a century of waiting in an empty castle for a master who never came back.

It looks at them. Focuses slowly. Like focusing is an effort it hasn't had to make in a long time.

Then, very quietly:

> _"Master....?"_

ADAM moves before Sephire can process what just happened. He doesn't draw a weapon. He steps directly into the spawn's reach — deliberate, controlled — and lets it take him. Grabs it as it grabs him. Uses its momentum. Drives it through the doorway of the adjacent room in a controlled crash.

The door swings shut behind them.

From the corridor Sephire hears violence. Contained. Professional. Brief.

From somewhere deeper in the castle — awakened by the noise — something else begins to move.

**Sephire fights alone:**

```encounter
name: Ravenloft Corridor
creatures:
 - 2: Ghoul
 - 1: Ghast
```

_Design note: This fight should feel isolating, not impossible. Sephire is level 8. But he's road-worn, 43 hours alone, and the castle smells like a century of abandonment. Let the environment do the work. The scorched guardian corpses in the courtyard — visible on the way in — are a clue Sephire can carry. Someone with significant divine power brought the dome down. It wasn't the party._

When ADAM emerges from the other room he is unhurt. He looks at the aftermath of Sephire's fight. Then at Sephire.

He says nothing about what happened behind that door.

He will never say anything about it.

He nods once — almost imperceptibly — and says:

> _"We should leave."_

**Cut to the road. Cut to the Strong Start. ADAM already knows what's inside Vallaki and is carrying the spawn's last word in whatever passes for his chest.**

### SCENE B — _The Tunnel_

**Who:** Sephire + ADAM **Location:** Eastern service tunnel beneath Vallaki's wall, then Uptown **Tone:** The most ADAM has ever talked. It costs him something visible.

The tunnel is Black Hood construction. Old enough that the builders are dead. The walls have registration numbers scratched into them at intervals — small, precise, going back decades. ADAM knows what they count. He doesn't explain.

He talks as they move. Not a speech. Just observations. Stated flatly. Without editorializing.

> _"The blood tax kept three thousand mortals alive last winter. Not comfortable. Alive."_

> _"The registration system has had four violent failures in a hundred years. Four. The century before it had hundreds."_

> _"HAWA looked at this plane and saw vampires controlling mortals. She was not wrong."_

Long pause. The tunnel narrows. They move single file.

When it widens again:

> _"She did not ask what the mortals were being controlled away from."_

The tunnel opens into a maintenance cellar beneath Uptown. ADAM stops at the bottom of the stairs leading up.

He turns and looks at Sephire directly. This is the second time he's done this. It means something that he does it twice.

> _"HAWA believes she is finishing something."_

> _"She is not."_

> _"She is beginning something."_

> _"There is a difference. She cannot see it."_

He gestures up the stairs.

> _"The Silent Inn. Central City. You know where the basement is."_

He's already moving back into the tunnel before Sephire reaches the top step.

Gone.

**Cut when Sephire steps out into Uptown and the city closes around him.**

### SCENE C — _The Loudest Inn_

**Who:** Froggo + Kairos + Asep **Location:** Second floor room, Loudest Inn, Lower City **Tone:** Coiled. Almost funny. Not funny.

Three Githyanki. Exhausted. Planes-worn. The kind of tired that comes from being hunted across multiple worlds.

They clock Froggo and Asep as non-human immediately. That buys thirty seconds of hesitation instead of an initiative roll.

The eldest — silver-eyed, scarred, two fingers missing from her left hand — looks past Froggo and Asep entirely.

She looks at Kairos.

She speaks in Gith.

Froggo and Asep understand nothing.

**What she says — for your notes only, never translated at the table:** _"Trevor's fire-mark. He said if we ever found it in the dark planes, give the bearer this: Scarlet V has a name in Barovia. The name is not a person. It is a system. And the system already knows you're here."_

Silence.

Froggo and Asep are watching Kairos's face.

**Asep beat:** If the Stone comes up in conversation — and it likely will, since Asep was the one who identified it as a Commune-type device — the eldest Githyanki goes very still. She looks at Asep directly for the first time. She says, in Common, carefully:

> _"Deep purple. Netherese cut. You have one of those here."_

Not a question. She knows what it is. Or knows enough to be frightened of it. She doesn't elaborate unless pressed — but the information is Asep's to chase if he wants it.

Whatever Kairos does with the Gith message — share it, deflect, lie, say nothing — the guard sweep gets louder. Boots on the stairs. Fist on the room next door.

The eldest looks at the door. Then at the party. Her two remaining fingers drum once on her knee.

She says, in Common, flatly:

> _"We have a shared problem."_

**The door starts to open.**

_Design note: How this resolves depends entirely on Kairos. If Rafi shares the message — the scene opens outward, the Githyanki become potential allies. If he stays silent — the scene closes inward, the shared problem gets solved mechanically and the weight stays buried in Rafi's chest where it's been since the Pequod left Shattar-Kai. Both outcomes are correct. Don't push either direction._

**Cut when the shared problem is established — not solved.**

### SCENE D — _The Rehearsal_

**Who:** Minerva (solo) **Location:** Mademoiselle's Opera Theatre, Grand Boulevard **Tone:** Beautiful. Surgical. The difference between those two things becoming visible.

Verdian stayed at the inn. Minerva came back alone because the letter asked for her specifically.

The rehearsal is different from the performance. More deliberate. Mademoiselle moves her physically — here, facing this angle, stars pulling in this direction. He's pleasant about it. Collaborative, even.

But Minerva is a healer. She reads bodies. She reads rooms.

**DC 13 Insight** — the sightlines aren't arranged for the audience to see her best. They're arranged so that when she performs, specific seats become the focal point of every other noble's gaze. She isn't the show. She's the lamp.

**DC 15 Investigation, looking backstage** — a seating chart. Names she doesn't recognize. Small symbols next to each one that match the insignia on the Black Hood operatives from HAWA's investigation board.

Mademoiselle notices her looking. He doesn't stop her. He watches with the expression of someone who wanted to see what she would do when she found it.

If she asks what the symbols mean he smiles — genuinely, not unkindly — and says:

> _"It means the show is for everyone, Doctor. Whether they know they're watching or not."_

A young dancer passes close and says quietly, not stopping:

> _"Mademoiselle says the show must go on regardless. Even if the city burns a little."_

**Cut when Minerva is asked to demonstrate her star manipulation directly above the seat designated for the Crown Prince — so Mademoiselle can check the angle.**

### SCENE E — _The Silent Inn, Upstairs_

**Who:** Verdian **Location:** The Silent Inn common room, Central City **Tone:** Ordinary. Then not.

Sign language only. Verdian is present. The inn is quiet the way inns are quiet when everyone in them is carefully not listening to anyone else.

A registered vampire enters. Young-looking. Human-passing except for the pallor and the wrist-mark — a number, slightly faded at the edges in a way wrist-marks shouldn't fade.

He looks around the common room. Finds Verdian. Approaches.

He signs. Badly. Like he learned recently, specifically for this. His hands shake slightly.

_"I need to speak to the elf woman. The one who lives beneath. I was told she helps people."_

He glances at his wrist. Back at Verdian.

_"My number has been fading for three days. I haven't told anyone. I didn't know who to tell."_

He waits.

**This is Verdian's moment. One decision. Does he bring this vampire downstairs or not?**

_Design note: Even if the player Asal Bunyi's this — even if he just points at the basement stairs — the scene carries weight. The fading number is the first visible face of the system's degradation in the city. This vampire trusted the compact. He followed every rule. His number is fading anyway. If Verdian actually engages, let it matter. The scene works either way._

**Cut when they reach the basement door.**

### SCENE F — _The Basement_

**Who:** HAWA — and whoever arrives **Location:** HAWA's safehouse, beneath The Silent Inn **Tone:** A woman who has done the math. The math is wrong. She doesn't know yet.

HAWA is updating the board when people arrive. Tired but not alarmed. The Stone sits on the desk next to her notes — not hidden, not secured. Just present. Like a tool between uses.

She speaks freely down here. That's the point of the basement.

She answers every question honestly. Every answer will be true. Some of them will be wrong in ways she cannot see. Never play her as nervous or suspicious. She has done the math and she is comfortable with the result.

**If shown the fading wrist-mark vampire:** She examines the mark carefully. Her expression shifts — not panic. Recalibration. She makes a note. She says the system has micro-fluctuations sometimes. It self-corrects. She believes this completely.

**If asked about the Stone:** She says it is safe. She has maintained all containment protocols. She has been careful. She believes this completely.

**If asked about the dome over Castle Ravenloft:** She confirms she brought it down. Selûne's power, channeled through her. She describes it as removing an obstacle. She does not describe it as a destabilization event. In her framework it was neither of those things. It was light entering a dark place.

**If asked about ADAM:** She says he is reliable. Then, after a pause — almost to herself:

> _"He has always been reliable."_

She does not say she trusts him. There is a difference she doesn't examine.

**Visible on her desk** — personal journal, one page open. Last line:

_"Selûne, let this be enough. Let what I have already done be enough."_

She doesn't know anyone can see it. She left it open because she has lived alone down here for six months and forgot what it means for someone else to be in the room.

**Cut when Sephire's boots are heard on the basement stairs.**

### SCENE G — _The Return_

**Who:** Full party, HAWA **Location:** HAWA's basement safehouse **Tone:** Everyone in the same room. Nobody knows what the person next to them is carrying.

Sephire walks in.

HAWA sees him. She knows he left for Ravenloft. She knows the castle is 18 hours away because she has been stationed here for six months and has done this calculation before. She knows what 43 hours of separation from Vallaki means.

She looks at him — not at what he might have brought back, but the way someone looks at a person who just walked through somewhere they consider irreversibly dangerous.

She says:

> _"You went to the castle."_

Not a question.

She already knows. She's establishing that she knows. Which establishes everything about what kind of conversation comes next.

**End the session here or let it breathe for one more exchange. Your table will tell you which.**

## Secrets & Clues

Ten things true in the world. Reveal **maximum three** this session. Let players find them. Don't deliver them.

1. The Githyanki's message — Scarlet V has structural presence in Barovia that predates the party's arrival. It is not a person.
2. Mademoiselle's seating chart maps every Black Hood operative in Vallaki by their noble proxy identity.
3. The Black Hoods are triangulating The Silent Inn specifically — not the Stone's magical signature, but HAWA's _behavioral pattern._ Six months of consistency is its own kind of signal.
4. The dome over Castle Ravenloft wasn't destroyed by the party's arrival. It fell months before. Someone with significant divine power brought it down deliberately. The scorched guardian corpses in the courtyard are the evidence.
5. The fading wrist-mark is not an isolated incident. There are others. The Black Hoods have been suppressing reports for weeks.
6. ADAM has contingency plans for every party member. He considers this standard administrative procedure. He does not consider it cruel.
7. The Festival is in two days. Every faction is accelerating because of it.
8. Mademoiselle and Alexander have been coordinating for three years. The Festival was always the target date. They chose it because it's the one night every Black Hood operative is publicly visible and accounted for.
9. The Crown Prince's bloodline has a documented history with Netherese artifacts not present in any library HAWA has accessed.
10. Kairos's silence has been noticed. Not by the party. By ADAM. Who has filed it as relevant to his contingency planning.

## Fantastic Locations

**The Eastern Service Tunnel** Black Hood construction. Old enough that the builders are dead. Registration numbers scratched into the walls at intervals going back decades. Smells of river water and old stone. The numbers count something ADAM knows and will not say.

**Castle Ravenloft Interior** A hundred years of abandonment in every surface. Identical rooms that once housed spawn. The courtyard holds scorched guardian corpses — evidence of the dome's destruction that HAWA caused and considers a victory. The spawn's last word — _Master_ — echoes differently in every room after it's been spoken.

**The Loudest Inn, Second Floor** Thin walls. The guard sweep audible from the street below. A room that makes secrets feel temporary and decisions feel permanent.

**Mademoiselle's Opera Theatre** Grand Boulevard. Rehearsal lighting makes everything look like performance. The seating chart backstage is pinned next to costume measurements. Both treated as equally logistical by the man who made them.

**The Silent Inn Common Room** Quiet enforced by social contract. Sign language only. A space where a shaking vampire with a fading wrist-mark has to explain a crisis with his hands to a Bard who may or may not be paying attention.

**HAWA's Basement Safehouse** Investigation board covering one full wall. Stone on the desk. Journal open. The one place in Vallaki where spoken word is allowed and the truth can surface. It looks like a command center. It is also a record of how much has already moved without her.

## Important NPCs

**ADAM** Dhampir. Lawful Neutral. Spoke more in the tunnel than he has in months. It cost him something and he won't repeat it. If asked about HAWA by a party member he says: _"She is sincere."_ He does not say she is right. He has already calculated the difference and found it significant.

**HAWA** Selûnite Cleric. Lawful Good. Tired. Certain. She has the Stone and she has her faith and she has not yet understood that she has been treating them as the same object. Play her as someone who has done the math and is comfortable with the result. Never nervous. Never suspicious. Uncomfortably, consistently, correct-sounding. She never lies. This is more dangerous than lying.

**The Elder Githyanki** No name given yet. Silver-eyed. Scarred. Two fingers missing from her left hand. She came here following Trevor's mark across multiple planes. She is not an enemy. She is a warning wearing a person. She spoke Gith to Kairos because she wanted only him to hear. She will help the party survive Vallaki if given a reason to stay. She has been given very few reasons to trust anyone recently.

**The Fading Vampire** Registered. Young-looking. Followed every rule. His number has been fading for three days and he came to The Silent Inn because someone told him the elf woman beneath helps people. He has a name if the party asks. He has a family if they ask further. He did not come here to be symbolic. He came because he is frightened and didn't know where else to go. He is the first face of the system's degradation. He did everything right.

**Mademoiselle** Always performing. Never unguarded. He chose Minerva because she is genuinely remarkable and genuinely uninvested in Vallaki's politics — the most credible spotlight he could find. He is not cruel. He is precise. When Minerva finds the seating chart he doesn't stop her because he wants to see what she does with it. That information is part of the show.

## How To End The Session

End when **one** of these happens — whichever comes first at your table:

- [ ] Kairos makes any choice about what the Githyanki told him
- [ ] Someone reads HAWA's journal line aloud or asks her about it
- [ ] Sephire tells another party member what ADAM said about HAWA in the tunnel

**Final line — choose your tone:**

_Quiet:_

> The Stone sits on HAWA's desk. Everyone who matters is finally in the same room. Nobody knows what the person next to them is carrying.

_Dread:_

> Somewhere in the Lower City, a vampire who followed every rule looks at his wrist in the dark. The number is almost gone.

_Weight:_

> Two days until the Festival. HAWA updates one last string on her board and steps back to look at it. It looks like an investigation. It is not an investigation. She doesn't know that yet.

## Last Note — For You, Not Them

Session 25 is about **accumulation and proximity.**

For the first time since Sephire left — everyone is back in the same basement. Same room. Same conversation.

And every single one of them is carrying something the others don't know.

Sephire has ADAM's words and the spawn's _"Master."_ Kairos has the Gith message stacked on top of what he's known since the Pequod left Shattar-Kai. Minerva has the seating chart and what it means about what she's actually being used for. Asep has a Githyanki who recognized the Stone and went still. Verdian has a vampire with a fading number who trusted the system completely and came to the right place and may or may not have been seen. Froggo has been watching all of it and understanding more than anyone has given him credit for.

Nobody puts it together this session.

That's not a failure of the session.

**That's the session working.**

The Festival is in two days. Let them carry the weight into it. Let it get heavier every hour between now and then.

You already built the bomb.

This session is everyone picking up a piece of it without knowing what it is.
