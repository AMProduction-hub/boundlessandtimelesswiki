## Session Engine
Structure: Intercut POV Scenes  
Tone: Rising tension under civilized surface  
Theme Question: "Who is actually in control here?"

Hard Rule:
Never resolve a scene fully before cutting.

Cut when:
- a secret is revealed
- danger appears
- a decision is demanded

5 — City lockdown announced  

Advance clock when:
- players stall
- players long rest
- table energy dips

### SCENE: Ravenloft — Sephire + Adam

Mood: Quiet danger, predator conversation

Adam tone = calm curiosity, not hostility.

Dialogue seed:
> "Strange… the righteous always walk into ruins alone."

Discoveries:
- Castle barrier destroyed by divine force
- Library untouched
- Everything else abandoned long ago
- Burned corpses of guardians

Trigger Combat When:
Sephire touches knowledge he shouldn’t

Enemy Role Design:
Controller + Bruiser hybrid creature

Purpose:
Teach Sephire: this castle still defends secrets.

Cut Scene When:
Creature drops below 40% HP  
→ cut to another POV before fight ends

---

### SCENE: Lower City Sweep

Atmosphere:
Doors shutting  
Windows closing  
People whispering  

Event:
Guards begin systematic search.

Clue Drop:
They are not searching randomly.  
They have a list.

Reveal if investigated:
Names on list = political dissidents, not criminals.

Choice Point:
Help Guild / Help Guards / Stay neutral

Cut when:
Players choose a side

---

### SCENE: Cabaret Performance

Tone: Beautiful. Wrong.

Details:
Music slightly too slow  
Audience too quiet  
Nobles watching performers like merchandise  

Hidden Truth:
Performers = leverage inventory.

Discovery Options:
- ledger book
- coded fan signals
- noble bidding gestures

Key Line (NPC whisper):
> “Power isn’t taken. It’s filmed.”

Cut when:
Minerva realizes she’s being evaluated, not applauded.

---

### SCENE: Ravenloft Disturbance

Trigger when clock hits 4.

Signs across all POVs:
- dogs howl
- moon flickers
- vampire marks glow faintly

Truth:
Something noticed Sephire entered the castle.

Do NOT explain what.

---

### SCENE: City Lockdown

Trigger at clock 5.

Announcement:
All gates sealed until festival.

Effect:
Sephire cannot return.

Player Emotion Goal:
“Oh. We’re separated for real.”

All three preparing for same event.

## ADAM RULES

He never lies.  
He never explains.

If Sephire asks motive:

## REVELATION LIMIT

Maximum truths players may learn this session: **2**

If they learn 2:
All other discoveries become partial or interrupted.

## EMERGENCY INSERT SCENES (Use if pacing drops)

• Child draws castle burning

## SESSION END TARGET

End when one happens:

□ Sephire learns castle is not abandoned  
□ Party realizes festival is dangerous  
□ Players suspect HAWA is being watched  

Whichever triggers first = ending scene.

Final Line Delivery (choose tone):

Uneasy:
> The city sleeps with one eye open.

Ominous:
> Somewhere in Vallaki, someone just gave an order.

Dread:
> Something has decided the party is important.
