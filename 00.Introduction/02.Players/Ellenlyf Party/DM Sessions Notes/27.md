## The Core Truth (DM Eyes Only)

The party was sent by the Bureau of Time and Plane to retrieve the 5th artifact from Barovia.

**The 5th artifact is [[Strahd von Zarovich]] himself.** He transformed into a stone — the Deep Purple Netherese Stone — now sitting on [[HAWA]]'s desk at The Silent Inn basement.

The Stone is simultaneously:

- Strahd's physical prison / transformed body
- A Commune device capable of commanding Selûne's physical manifestation (the moon)
- The keystone of Barovia's entire vampire registration and coexistence system
- The object the Bureau needs to complete Ao's plan to absorb divine power

**The Moon above Barovia is Selûne's anchored body.** She has been imprisoned here for a hundred years — not by malice but by the Stone's signal maintaining her presence as infrastructure. She is conscious. She has been watching. The moonbeam wasn't an attack. It was a flinch.

**[[HAWA]] using the Stone at the Festival:**

1. Burned every registered vampire standing in the open — vaporized on contact
2. Burned mortals caught in prolonged exposure — the Festival crowd
3. Broke the registration signal — every wrist-mark went dark simultaneously
4. Yanked Selûne's anchor chain — she responded with a century of restrained fury
5. Cracked the barrier between Selûne's full presence and this plane — briefly

**Nobody in the party caused this. Nobody in the party stopped it. They are accessories by presence and by the accumulated weight of six months of not knowing enough soon enough.**

## Post-Festival State (After T=0)

_Replace the Festival Clock with this section once T=0 has happened at the table._

### What Happened

HAWA raised the Stone at the Festival opening ceremony. She said a prayer. She meant every word.

The moon moved to direct overhead position — not a beam descending but the moon itself repositioned, its light total and vertical and inescapable across the Festival grounds. No shadow geometry from buildings provided relief. No angle of incidence. Just the light, from directly above, on everything.

**Registered vampires in the open:** Vaporized. Not burned — _gone._ The ones who trusted the compact enough to celebrate in the street are the ones who aren't there anymore.

**Mortals in the open:** First second — warmth. Then intensity. Sustained contact burns. The Festival crowd was packed. Movement was slow. Some people did not make it to shadow in time.

**The registration signal:** Broke the instant the moon moved from its anchor position. Every wrist-mark in Vallaki went dark simultaneously. Every surviving vampire felt a hundred years of managed hunger snap free.

**Selûne:** Responded. The crack in the anchor — the first crack in a century — produced a fragment of her presence. Not an avatar in the traditional sense. Something between a prisoner whose chains were yanked and a goddess who has been watching her own body used as infrastructure. She appeared in the rubble for approximately thirty seconds and spoke one thing to whoever was nearest:

> _"The stone must not leave this plane."_

Then she was gone. The crack closed. The anchor remains — the Stone still exists.

**[[Verdian Suyanti|Venty]]:** Was in HAWA's basement with three fading vampires when T=0 hit. Two vaporized where they sat — the basement receives indirect light through the stairwell. The third survived. Venty is alone in the basement with the Stone on the desk and what remains of two people he had been sitting with for hours.

**The party:** Accessories. They didn't pull the trigger. They also didn't stop it. The weight of the distinction is theirs to carry.

## The Three Hunts (Post-Festival)

_These replace the Festival Clock as the primary world pressure. All three run simultaneously._

---

### Hunt 1 — The Black Hood Society

**What they want:** Stability restored. The Stone recovered. Someone blamed.

**What happened to them:** Radovan's report was complete before the Festival. The response team was mobilized. Then Vallaki burned and their Castle records room may have been destroyed by Adrianna's operation simultaneously. They have lost leverage over the nobility. They have lost the registration system. They have lost eighty years of public peace.

**What they know about the party:** Foreign outsiders. No documentation. Present during the lockdown. Connected to the elf woman who had the Stone. That's enough.

**What they'll do:** Issue warrants through the Crown Prince's guard structure. Use Radovan's documentation to build a case. Make Vallaki operationally hostile through every official channel available.

**Escalation if ignored:** Response team moves from administrative pressure to direct action. Radovan is no longer their investigator. He becomes something else.

**Reaction if they learn the Stone is Strahd:** Radovan goes very still. The report gets significantly more complicated. The Black Hood Society pauses — not from sympathy, but because Strahd returning would be worse for them than anything the party has done.

---

### Hunt 2 — The Pantheons

**What they want:** The artifacts not delivered to Ao. The party redirected — not killed, redirected.

**What's happening:** The gods and goddesses affected by Ao's plan have been watching since Artifact 1. The Barovia incident — Selûne's body yanked, her anchor cracked, a fragment of her presence forced through — is the moment they decide waiting is no longer viable.

**How they operate:** They don't send armies. They send _agents._ Divine champions. Celestials. Chosen. Beings who don't belong in this plane, waiting at crossroads, watching from rooftops, appearing at the edges of maps.

**The first contact:** Selûne's fragment already happened. The party has met one goddess — partially, briefly, in rubble. More will follow.

**What they know about the party:** Everything. The six artifacts. Ao's plan. The party's composition and history. That Kairos has known since the Pequod and said nothing.

**Escalation:** The agents become more visible. More insistent. Eventually the party will have to engage — refuse them, ally with them, or navigate between them and the Bureau simultaneously.

**The question this raises for Kairos:** A god just said out loud what he has been carrying alone since the Pequod. His silence is no longer sustainable. The Pantheon agents will speak to him specifically. The party will see it.

---

### Hunt 3 — Their Own Conscience

_The softest hunt. The one that never stops._

The party had the information. The conspiracy board was built in Session 23 — they connected the strings themselves. ADAM told Sephire directly. Venty moved a string for aesthetic reasons and created visual proof sitting in the basement.

They didn't get there in time. Or didn't try hard enough. The distinction depends on how honest they're being with themselves — and that is not a question you answer for them.

Vallaki isn't all of Barovia. The Festival grounds and the immediate streets are the disaster zone. But the Festival grounds are where the mortals who trusted the compact were standing next to the vampires who trusted the compact when the light came down.

Alice was in that crowd. Minerva covered her with her robes on the stairs of the Castle. She walked through a door and didn't go back.

This hunt has no escalation mechanic. It just sits there. In the quiet between scenes.

## Selûne's Fragment — The Avatar

_This scene happens in the rubble after T=0. Not during the chaos. In the quiet after._

She appears to whoever is nearest. Not the classic silver-haired goddess imagery — that's the version people pray to. This is Selûne as she actually is: partially present, partially elsewhere, her edges uncertain like she isn't quite fully rendered in this plane. Her light is real. Her form is translucent. When she speaks it feels like you're remembering her words rather than hearing them.

She looks at whoever is nearest for approximately thirty seconds.

Then:

> _"The stone must not leave this plane."_

Gone. The crack closes.

**What this does to the party:**

- Confirms something conscious is connected to the Stone
- Gives a direct divine instruction contradicting the Bureau mission
- Makes Kairos's silence completely unsustainable
- Gives HAWA the opportunity to display the full depth of her certainty

**HAWA's response if told:** She goes quiet for a moment. Then:

> _"Selûne's will is to banish darkness. A fragment of her presence, filtered through a damaged anchor, in the moment of her greatest expenditure — that is not a clear communication. That is an echo."_

She believes this. She is not lying. She is wrong.

## HAWA's Revelation — The Pantheons Brief

_HAWA delivers this as tactical information in the aftermath. Not as confession._

HAWA tells the party:

The Pantheons are active. Gods and goddesses across multiple planes have been aware of Ao's project — the collection of artifacts, the absorption of divine power. They have been building toward intervention. The party, by holding Artifact 5, has made themselves visible to divine attention in a way that cannot be undone.

Ao has not acted directly yet. He sent contractors — the party — and waited. The Pantheons interpret this restraint as preparation. They are not wrong.

The party needs to understand what they're carrying and what that makes them. They are targets now. Not just of the Black Hood Society. Of desperate gods.

**What she doesn't say:** That she caused this. That she was wrong. That the mortals in the street burned because of her.

**The scene's power:** She is giving completely accurate strategic intelligence while standing in the rubble of what she did. She sounds like a commander debriefing after a difficult operation. The players will hear both simultaneously. That is the scene.

## The Arc's Exit Condition

_The party leaves Barovia when staying becomes more dangerous than going._

This is not a scripted ending. It is a sandbox condition created by the three hunts converging:

- The Black Hood Society makes Vallaki operationally hostile through official channels
- Pantheon agents become too visible to ignore
- HAWA's continued presence and certainty creates an impossible situation — she will try to use the Stone again if she keeps it
- Sephire arrives with everything ADAM told him and everything the mummified spawn revealed — information the party needs to make a final decision about the Stone

**The exit isn't escape. It's the party choosing to take the problem with them because it cannot be resolved here.**

**What they carry out of Barovia:**

- The Stone — keeping it, handing it to the Bureau, or something else is the last choice of the arc
- The knowledge of what the Bureau actually is and what Ao is building
- Kairos's secret — finally surfaced because it had to be
- Whatever they carry from Alice, from the Festival crowd, from HAWA in the rubble
- Selûne's words: _the stone must not leave this plane_

**They arrive in Sigil at the start of Act 2 as people who know what they're part of.** Not contractors. Not heroes. Something more complicated and more honest than either.

Trevor Lohefather is waiting for Kairos. The Githyanki commander who escaped via Plane Shift is somewhere in the Astral. The Bureau will want a debrief. The Pantheon agents will follow them through the gate. Act 2 begins not with a fresh start but with the weight of Act 1 walking in through the door ahead of them.

## The Tunnel Network

Beneath Vallaki. ADAM has been using and maintaining it for decades.

**Entry points:**

- The Silent Inn basement → Central City corridor
- Eastern wall service tunnel → Uptown (shown to Sephire, Session 25)
- Whale oil lamppost hatch → Guild's sewer entry (Froggo and Kairos, Session 26)
- ADAM's private access → beneath Vallaki Castle's foundation

**The Sewer Complex map (Dyson Logos):** Froggo and Kairos entered from the upper section — Guild operational zone. Sephire entered from the lower section — ADAM's private infrastructure tunnels. Same physical network. Different ends. May or may not cross paths depending on player choices.

**The registration infrastructure:** Physical mechanism of the wrist-mark system in the deeper tunnels. Old Netherese construction. Maintained by ADAM personally. Now offline. His maintenance logs in handwriting going back decades are still there.

## The Sandbox Principles

**The world moves at the Festival Clock pace regardless of player action.** After T=0 the world moves at the pace of the three hunts. All three are always active simultaneously.

**Every NPC has a want they pursue.** They don't wait for the party.

**Player actions have faction consequences.** Every choice feeds into at least one hunt. There is no neutral action anymore.

**The Spec Ops mirror is always running.** Walker didn't know he was the catastrophe until it was too late. Your players know now. Act 2 asks: _what do you do with that knowledge?_

**Alice is already gone.** It will surface when it surfaces.

**Selûne said one thing.** The party heard it. HAWA will reframe it. The Bureau will ignore it. The Pantheons will repeat it in different forms until the party decides.
