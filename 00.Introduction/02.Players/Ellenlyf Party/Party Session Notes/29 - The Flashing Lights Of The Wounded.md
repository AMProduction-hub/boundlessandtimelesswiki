### Sephire's Perspective — T=30 Minutes to Festival

[[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] let [[Sephire]] through.

Not as a concession. As a calculation. [[Sephire]] was one person, road-worn, without the party, heading into a city that was about to become very complicated. Radovan apparently decided that was acceptable.

[[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] was not permitted to enter.

Radovan told him directly — [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] carried Strahd's bloodline and was bound by contract not to enter Vallaki's boundaries. Whether the contract was old Bureau paperwork or something the Black Hood Society had arranged decades ago wasn't explained. The boundary was stated. [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] accepted it without argument, which said something about how long that contract had been in place.

[[Sephire]] asked Radovan about the situation inside the city and about his party members. Radovan confirmed the Festival was underway and that people matching the party's descriptions had last been located in the sewer network beneath the Lower City.

[[Sephire]] left [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] at the gate.

It took approximately ninety minutes of navigating toward the city center before [[Sephire]] found a drainage tunnel exit into the market area. He looked through the grate and saw Vallaki in full Festival mode — mortals and registered vampires together in the streets, music, decorations, the particular energy of a city that does this every year and has learned to mean it.

He emerged. He oriented himself. The Silent Inn was the first destination — that was where the party regrouped, where [[HAWA]]'s safehouse was, where the investigation board with Venty's string still connected _Outsiders Arrived_ to _Moon Behavior Changes._

He was walking toward it when the moon above Vallaki moved.

Not set. Not drifted. _Moved_ — deliberately, purposefully, repositioning overhead with the specific quality of something being commanded rather than something obeying natural physics.

The light changed. [[Sephire]] felt it before he saw it — a warmth that shouldn't have been there at this hour, intensifying faster than warmth should intensify.

**DC 16 Constitution save. He failed.**

The wave of light hit him outside the Silent Inn. He went down.

_Session ends on [[Sephire]]'s perspective here._

### Vallaki Sewer — T=11 Hours to Festival

#### The Final Conversation in the Hideout

The debate about the Stone had been running for a while when it crystallized into its real shape.

[[Alexander|Adrianna/Alexander]] wanted to use it. Her reasoning was the same reasoning it had always been — if the moon's light once burned vampires and something suppressed that, something could restore it. The Stone was the most obvious candidate for both the suppression and the restoration. The Black Hood Society's grip on Vallaki had survived everything the Guild threw at it for years. This was the last option she could see.

The party's resistance came from experience rather than theory. Every plane they had collected an artifact from, something structural had broken. They had pattern recognition even without complete information. Using the Stone felt like pulling a pin and hoping for the best.

[[Froggo]] was the one who said it plainly:

> _"Based on everything we've seen — if we use the Stone, something in this world breaks. But if Barovia stays like this, isn't that also a world that's broken?"_

No clean answer. The Stone stayed as a last resort.

[[HAWA]], who had been awake for over twenty hours and had not stopped working, finally told them what she knew:

The Stone could be used to control the moon. She said this without looking up from her notes. She said they needed to find another way. She did not say she had already decided what the other way was.

She had been awake for twenty hours. She was still writing.

---

#### The Door

The hidden door mechanism triggered.

Not dramatically. Just the sound of something mechanical on the other side — the specific sound of someone who knew what they were looking for and had found it.

[[Alexander|Adrianna]] was on her feet before the sound finished.

She looked at [[HAWA]]. Then at the party. Then at the route that led deeper into the sewer network — away from the door, away from whoever was on the other side.

> _"Go."_

No speech. No last words about what it was all for. The calculation had already been made — it had been made years ago, probably, when she first took the Alexander moniker and understood what the title eventually asked of its holders.

Her team positioned. The party moved.

Behind them — the sound of the door giving way. The sound of engagement. Then, after a while, those sounds stopped.

[[Alexander|Adrianna]] mentioned before the door opened that the ones coming were led by her twin sister. [[Sasha Vegelberg]] — unregistered vampire, Black Hood operative — had found them.

---

#### The Ambush

The secret route out of the hideout looped back toward familiar territory — the section of tunnel where the Basilisk corpses had been concealed.

Whoever sent the Black Hood response team knew the sewer network. They were already waiting at the exit point.

The fight was bad.

[[Kairos]], [[Verdian Suyanti|Venty]], and [[Froggo]] had been running on depleted resources since the Castle escape. The vampire response team hit them at the worst possible moment — exhausted, outnumbered, in a tunnel with limited maneuvering room.

Several party members came close to going down. [[Froggo]] was critically wounded.

[[HAWA]] used her **Divine Intervention.**

Whatever she asked of Selûne in that moment — whatever prayer she sent up through twenty hours of certainty and exhaustion — it was answered. The intervention bought the party the space they needed.

[[Minerva]] made the call. She cast [[polymorph|Polymorph]] on [[Froggo]].

A Tyrannosaurus Rex filled the tunnel.

[[Froggo]], now approximately forty feet of ancient predator, had disadvantage on everything because his new body's posture was deeply wrong — centuries of frog instinct translating poorly into theropod architecture. He was slouching. A slouching T-Rex is still a T-Rex. He carried the party out.

They ran. The vampires did not follow into the section of tunnel a T-Rex had just vacated.

---

#### [[Kairos]]'s Note

_Written in [[Kairos]]'s own words:_

> _On the hideout, we discussed the stone and the moon once more. Our hideout got found and we were on our way to hide but got found out. We fight the vampires, almost dead multiple times, a T-Rex shows up somehow. Got flashed. Died, undied, then we hid to safety._

The flash came through the drainage grates when the party was deep enough in the tunnels to feel it rather than see it directly. A pulse of light from above — wrong quality, wrong intensity, the moon doing something the moon doesn't do.

[[Kairos]] went down. Then came back up.

He did not say what Damian told him on the Pequod.

The party found a section of the sewer [[HAWA]] had prepared for exactly this contingency — stocked, maintained, hidden. They took their long rest there.

Above them, the Festival was still going.

## Key Learnings

- **[[HAWA]] confirmed the Stone can control the moon.** She said they needed to find another way. She had been awake for over twenty hours and did not stop working after saying it.
- **[[Alexander|Adrianna/Alexander]] held the door.** The response team that found the hideout was led by [[Sasha Vegelberg]] — Adrianna's twin sister and Black Hood operative. Adrianna and her remaining team bought the party time. Whether they survived is unknown.
- **[[HAWA]] used Divine Intervention** during the vampire ambush. She asked Selûne for something in her most desperate moment. Selûne answered.
- **[[Froggo]] was Polymorphed into a Tyrannosaurus Rex** by [[Minerva]] and carried the party out of the ambush. He had disadvantage on everything because he was slouching.
- **[[Kairos]] did not tell the party what Damian told him.** The Stone is what Ao will use to absorb the power of all the gods and goddesses. Kairos has known since Shattar-Kai. He stayed silent.
- **[[Sephire]] was hit by the moonwave** outside the Silent Inn at T=30 minutes. Failed DC 16 Constitution save. He went down. This was before the party emerged from the sewers — he felt what was coming before anyone else.
- **[[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] cannot enter Vallaki.** Bound by contract — confirmed by [[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] at the gate. He is outside the walls. He felt the moon move from there.
- **The party is taking a long rest** in [[HAWA]]'s prepared sewer section. The Festival is above them. T=0 is coming.
