### Sephire's Perspective — T=3 Hours to Festival

[[Sephire]] reached the outer walls of Vallaki and found the main gate sealed — iron bars down, guards visible through the grating. The Festival lockdown was in effect. Nobody in, nobody out.

[[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] was already moving before [[Sephire]] finished assessing the gate. He transformed into a swarm of bats and circled once, signaling with a wing-gesture to follow. They moved away from the main road and worked around the outer wall toward the lake.

When they were clear of sight [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] reverted and delivered the situation plainly:

_The Festival lockdown goes into effect the day before every year. Standard procedure. The main gate won't open until after the ceremony._

But there was another way in. Through the drainage system at the lake's edge. A route [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] had used before — had built, in parts, and maintained quietly. He pointed toward the water.

They took a sampan across to the drainage entrance.

On the water, [[Sephire]] asked about the Festival and what came next. [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] answered in the way he always answered — not evasively, but precisely:

1. The lockdown happens every year, approximately one day before the Festival opens. This was normal.
2. The Festival is the ceremony for the new leader of Vallaki — the annual reaffirmation of the Royal bloodline's rule.
3. The book's reference to _"a piece of Selûne's body"_ being required for the ritual — [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] paused here, then said it was likely a poetic metaphor. His face turned toward the moon as he said it.

He didn't elaborate. [[Sephire]] filed the pause.

They reached the drainage entrance and [[Sephire]] decided to rest before entering — eighteen hours of riding followed by however long the tunnels would take. He needed to be functional when he arrived. [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] agreed without comment and settled into stillness nearby while [[Sephire]] slept.

When they were ready to enter, a man was waiting at the drainage entrance.

Well-dressed. Pale. Middle-aged in appearance. He stepped toward them with the unhurried confidence of someone who had been waiting comfortably for some time, and said:

> _"What a coincidence — meeting someone so interesting. Who else but the pure-blooded descendant of Strahd himself. Adam."_

[[Radovan Czeslav Voss]]. Fully reverted. Unhurt. With his second notebook.

He had not come to report. He had already reported. He had come to say the name out loud, in front of [[Sephire]], where [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] would have to hear it and respond.

Whatever passed between [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] and [[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] in that moment — whatever calculation [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] made about what this cost him — he did not show it.

[[Radovan Czeslav Voss]] informed them, pleasantly, that the Black Hood Society was aware of the situation. That whatever happened at the Festival would be documented. That consequences were already in motion and not dependent on his personal presence.

Then he left. Back into the city. Not pursued.

[[Sephire]] and [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] entered the drainage tunnels.

### Vallaki Sewer — T=25 Hours to Festival

#### Arriving at the First Hideout

After escaping the Castle, the whole party plus [[HAWA]] moved through the sewer network until [[HAWA]] stopped at what appeared to be a blank section of wall and pressed something that wasn't visible unless you were looking for it. A door opened into a room that was genuinely comfortable by sewer standards — kept, maintained, with supplies that suggested someone had been stocking it for exactly this kind of emergency.

[[HAWA]] had prepared for this. Of course she had.

They settled in and those who could, slept.

---

#### Froggo's Dream

[[Froggo]] dreamed of darkness without edges — no floor, no ceiling, no direction. Then above him, from what he assumed was the sky, the moon appeared. Brighter than it had any right to be. Brighter than [[Froggo]] had ever seen it in centuries of living.

A voice came with it. Not from anywhere specific — more like it arrived directly in his mind, bypassing his ears entirely. Feminine. Unfamiliar. And it said:

> _"Sepertinya ini adalah waktunya aku bercahaya."_ _"It seems this is the time for me to shine."_

Then [[Froggo]] felt himself falling and woke up.

The room was unchanged. Across from him [[HAWA]] was still at her notes, completely absorbed, showing no sign of having slept.

---

#### HAWA's Half-Truth

[[Froggo]] approached [[HAWA]] and they talked while the others slept. She confirmed the shape of things — the Festival was the Crown Prince's annual reaffirmation ceremony, the Black Hood Society had been running the city through the royal puppet for generations, the Guild and [[HAWA]] had been trying to dismantle that structure, and now they were being hunted for it.

When the conversation came around to the [[Deep Purple Netherstone]] and what to do with it, [[HAWA]] said:

> _"The Stone can be used to manipulate the moon. That much I know. But we need to find another way."_

She said it without looking up from her notes.

She had been awake for over twenty hours. She did not stop working.

---

#### Moving to the Guild's Position

The party relocated through the sewer network toward the Thieves Guild's deeper hideout. The tunnels were navigable but increasingly unfamiliar — deeper sections that the party hadn't seen before, older construction, less maintained.

Before they reached the Guild's position they heard something ahead. Not footsteps. Something heavier and slower, with the particular quality of a creature that has nowhere else to go.

Two [[basilisk|Basilisks]]. Occupying the tunnel section between the party and their destination.

[[Froggo]] held up his hand and stepped forward alone. He had centuries. He had patience. He had diplomatic instincts that predated this city.

He spoke to them.

The Basilisks listened. They were hungry — something had disturbed their usual territory, pushed them into this section of the sewer, and they hadn't fed in too long. [[Froggo]] offered them an alternative: people would be coming through these tunnels. They didn't have to fight the party. They could wait and eat the others.

One of the Basilisks considered this.

Then it told [[Froggo]] that he and his companions would also satisfy their hunger just fine.

[[Froggo]] shot it with an arrow.

The fight was not long but it was not free. When it was over the party spent time cleaning up — masking the corpses, dispersing the blood smell, doing what they could to make the section look undisturbed. Whatever had pushed the Basilisks in here was still out there. No reason to advertise that the party had been through.

---

#### The Thieves Guild — What Was Left

Five people. The rest of the Guild was captured, scattered, or in hiding above ground, embedded in Festival positions they could no longer safely abandon.

The real [[Alexander|Adrianna/Alexander]] was there. This was the first time most of the party had seen her without a crowd around her — in a low-ceilinged sewer room with five exhausted operatives and the knowledge that her operation had played out under circumstances nobody planned for.

She knew about the Stone. She suggested using it.

The party pushed back. From everything they had experienced — from Shattar-Kai, from the Frontier, from the Bureau's pattern — anything that involved using the Stone ended in structural damage to the plane it was used in. The Barovia arc was five sessions of watching that principle in slow motion.

[[HAWA]] added that the Stone was more like a communication device than a weapon — it reached toward something, it spoke to something, it was a channel rather than a hammer. What it communicated with and how remained unclear.

The Stone stayed off the table. A last resort at most.

[[Alexander|Adrianna]] then shared something she had been carrying for a while — information about the moon's history in Barovia:

When the moon first appeared above the plane, vampire skin burned in the moonlight. Then the moonlight dimmed. And nobody had ever been able to explain why — not the vampires, not the mortal historians, not the Black Hood Society who had been administering the plane for eighty years.

The party did not immediately connect this to the Stone. But the information sat in the room with everything else.

The Black Hood Society knows who [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] is.

His face turned toward the moon as he said it. He knows. He has not said what he knows directly.
- **[[Froggo]] received Selûne's dream** — a voice directly in his mind, the moon blazing above him in darkness: _"It seems this is the time for me to shine."_ The source was unidentifiable but feminine and unfamiliar. This is the Pantheon's second contact with the party.
- **[[HAWA]] said the Stone can manipulate the moon but they need to find another way.** She has been awake for over twenty hours and has not stopped working. She did not look up from her notes when she said it.
- **When the moon first appeared over Barovia, moonlight burned vampires.** Then it stopped. Nobody knows why. Alexander confirmed this as historical fact.
- **The Basilisks were displaced** — something disturbed their usual territory and pushed them into this section of the sewer. The party killed them and cleaned up. Whatever pushed them is still in the tunnels.
- **The Thieves Guild is down to five members.** The operation is effectively over. Alexander is present and knows about the Stone.
- **[[Sephire]] is in the drainage tunnels with [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]]** heading toward the party. He carries the book, the mummified creature's revelation, and everything [[ADAM|Warga Lokal]] told him on the road.
