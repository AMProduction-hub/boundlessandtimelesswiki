# HAWA

## Overview

HAWA is a senior Selûnite cleric who arrived in Barovia six months before the party. She is the Bureau of Time and Plane's stationed agent for the Barovia retrieval mission. She has been the party's guide, their host, their source of information about the plane.

She is also the source of most of the damage.

She did not build the coexistence system. She arrived to find it, decided it was wrong, and began dismantling it — through investigation, through cultivating contacts, through breaking the dome over Castle Ravenloft with Selûne's power, and most significantly through taking the **Deep Purple Netherstone** <!-- FIXME [[Deep Purple Netherstone]] not in CHARACTER_SLUGS --> from its original location and carrying it around Vallaki for six months.

She has been unwittingly powering and destabilizing the system simultaneously. The registration marks fading on vampires' wrists are fading fastest along her daily walking routes. The moon above Barovia tracks her movement. She does not know any of this.

She believes she is finishing something righteous.

She is beginning something catastrophic.

She is the most dangerous person in Barovia. Not because she is cruel. Because she is certain.

---

## The Corrected Overview

**She is NOT the architect of the post-Strahd system.** That was Strahd himself — through the Stone — and ADAM's hundred years of maintenance.

**She arrived and saw vampires controlling mortals** through the blood tax and registration system and concluded this was wrong. She was not wrong about what she saw. She was wrong about what it was _for._

**She broke the Ravenloft dome** using Selûne's power six months ago. This woke the mummified Vampire Spawn in the library and made Castle Ravenloft accessible for the first time in decades. She considers this a victory. It was the beginning of everything unraveling.

**She took the Stone** believing it was a containment mechanism she needed to understand. She has been carrying Strahd's transformed body in her bag ever since.

**She has read the secondary text** in the Castle library confirming her theory about the Stone. She now believes she knows exactly how to use it at the Festival. She is going to use it to call Selûne's moonbeam down on Vallaki as a blessing.

She will be yanking on Selûne's anchor chain. The response will not be a blessing.

## Personal Traits

- Speaks calmly. Never raises her voice. This is its own kind of pressure.
- Frames every decision as an act of compassion. Genuinely believes this framing.
- Deeply distrusts the idea of exceptional individuals — including herself, theoretically.
- Genuinely mourns every life lost. Including those she has caused.
- Incapable of seeing herself as a tyrant.
- When confronted with evidence against her position, she absorbs it and reframes it as confirmation that the work is harder than expected.

---

## Beliefs & Ideology

- Selûne's light reveals what is hidden. Darkness must be exposed.
- Vampires are inherently cyclical threats regardless of current behavior.
- Peace is not natural — it must be maintained continuously.
- If suffering is inevitable, it should be minimized and controlled.
- She is not judging anyone. She is preparing for what they will become.

## Key Dialogue Notes

HAWA should never monologue unless pressed. Her words are most dangerous when they are calm and brief.

> _"If not us, then who will carry the burden?"_

> _"You call this coexistence. I call it a delay."_

> _"Evil does not announce itself. It waits until mercy grows tired."_

> _"I am not judging them. I am preparing for what they will become."_

> _"I know what it does now. I know how to use it properly."_ _(said after reading the secondary text — Session 26)_

> _"I told you everything that was true. I simply didn't tell you everything."_ _(if confronted about her omissions)_

> _"I would do it again. Because the alternative is that they suffer in the dark forever and call it peace."_ _(if shown the consequences of her actions)_

## GM Notes (Hidden)

**The core truth about HAWA:** She is not evil. She is not lying. She is not corrupt. She is a true believer whose faith has become a weapon she doesn't know she's holding. Every single thing she has told the party is technically true. She withholds context, not facts. This is the most dangerous version of her.

**She never lies.** This is both her defining virtue and her defining flaw. She answers honestly and completely about what she knows. She does not know enough. And she cannot be convinced she doesn't know enough because her certainty is theological, not empirical.

**The white phosphorus moment:** When HAWA uses the Stone at the Festival and calls Selûne's moonbeam down on Vallaki — she will stand in the aftermath and see a liberation. She will grieve the casualties. She will not stop believing she was right. This is the scene the entire arc has been building toward.

**If the party tries to take the Stone:** She will not stop them by force. She will say: _"Then you are choosing the darkness."_ And she will mean it. And she will begin preparing Barovia for the consequences without their consent.

**If shown the Guide of Selune Bodies Manipulation:** She already took notes from it in Castle Ravenloft before the party arrived. It confirms her plan. She will not be dissuaded by it — she will use it as justification.

**If told the Stone is Strahd:** She does not believe it. She requires extraordinary proof. Even then she will rationalize it — if Strahd is inside the Stone, that is more reason to use it to call Selûne's power, not less. Selûne's light would purify him. She is certain of this.

**Her faith in Selûne is sincere, not political.** She has been a Selûnite since before she could walk. The goddess is not a tool she uses — the goddess is the lens through which she sees everything. Including this.
